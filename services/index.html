Start with the problem you have

Services are grouped by what shows up first.

Water not draining

Slow sinks, backed-up tubs, and gurgling toilets. Start with drain cleaning, and for stubborn buildup consider hydro jetting.

Sewer problems

Odors, floor drains backing up, or repeat clogs. Sewer camera inspection shows the pipe, then cleaning or repair follows.

Water where it should not be

Leaks, wet walls, and bills that jumped. Try leak detection first, or burst pipe repair when a line has split.

Hot water and heat

Repair, replacement, installation, tankless units, and boiler repair.

Basement water

A pit that will not pump or seepage after rain. See sump pumps and waterproofing.

Fixtures and new work

Toilets, faucets, disposals, installations, repiping, and commercial plumbing.

Choosing between similar services

Many problems sit between two services, and the right pick depends on what the water is doing. A single slow sink usually needs drain cleaning at the fixture, while a gurgle across several fixtures points to the main line and may call for hydro jetting or a camera look first. A water heater that is cold may need a part, or it may be at the end of its life, and the age on the label helps decide.

A damp basement is another example. If water arrives through a floor drain, the work is on the drain and sewer side. If it arrives through the wall after rain, sump pump and waterproofing work is more likely to help. Describing the symptom on the phone saves a wasted trip, and we will say if the real fix is something we do not do.

Where a job needs a permit, New Jersey's plumbing subcode adopts the 2021 National Standard Plumbing Code. We tell you when a permit is likely and send you to the Westfield construction office to confirm.

Gas and other limits

If you smell gas, leave the building and call PSE&G or 911 before you call a plumber. Permit rules vary by job, so ask the Westfield construction office before work starts. For details on licenses and how we work, see the about page.
