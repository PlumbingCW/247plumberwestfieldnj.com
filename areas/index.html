Westfield neighborhoods and landmarks

Westfield covers about 6.7 square miles. Downtown, around Mindowaskin Park, East Broad Street, Elm Street, and the train station on the NJ Transit Raritan Valley Line, is the oldest part and was settled in 1720. The South Avenue traffic circle, Willow Grove Road, and Route 28 along the rail line are the roads we plan around, and US Route 22 runs just north.

The town's preservation plan names districts such as Stoneleigh Park, Wychwood, Dudley Park, and Prospect Street, and parks such as Tamaques, Brightwood, Gumbert, and Echo Lake. In older neighborhoods, mention the age of the house when you call, since it affects what we expect behind the walls. Lenape Park, about 450 acres shared with Springfield, Kenilworth, and Cranford, follows the Rahway River and Nomahegan Brook, so homes near the water should know where their basement drain goes.

Which towns sit where

Westfield borders Mountainside to the north, Springfield Township to the northeast, Garwood and Cranford to the east, Clark to the southeast, and Scotch Plains to the west and southwest. Fanwood is a nearby borough, and the Plainfield ZIPs 07060 to 07063 are a few miles southwest.

Our page on emergency plumbing in Scotch Plains covers the township's flood history and basement concerns in detail. For the work itself, see our plumbing services and our drain cleaning and sump pump pages, which many nearby homeowners start with.

Water and storms across the area

Flooding does not stop at a town line. Tropical Storm Ida in September 2021 brought catastrophic flooding to Westfield, and Scotch Plains saw upwards of 10 inches of rain. A July 2025 storm dropped more than six inches on the Westfield area by 8 p.m., with Scotch Plains reporting 7.9 inches. Basements, sump pits, and floor drains in every nearby town were tested.

Robinson's Branch, a tributary of the Rahway River, runs through the southern part of Westfield and through Scotch Plains toward the Robinson's Branch Reservoir in Clark. If you live near it, check your sump pump before storm season and keep floor drains clear. Call 811 before any digging near a drain or sewer line.
